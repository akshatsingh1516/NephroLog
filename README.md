## 1) App Description

NephroLog is an iOS app built to support people managing chronic kidney disease (CKD), including dialysis patients, transplant recipients, and users supported by caregivers.

The app helps users organize essential daily routines in one place. It includes fluid tracking, kidney-focused nutrition logging, medication reminders, and appointment planning for dialysis sessions, specialist visits, and lab follow-ups.

Core capabilities:
- Fluid intake tracking against daily goals
- Medication scheduling with reminder support
- Nutrition logging focused on kidney-relevant nutrients such as sodium, potassium, phosphorus, and protein
- Appointment management for treatment and follow-up care
- Emergency-ready health profile access for critical information

NephroLog is designed for clarity and consistency, so users can log information quickly and maintain adherence over time.

Medical disclaimer: NephroLog is a self-management support tool and does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
